About My Work

I began drawing at a very young age as a way to entertain myself as an only-child. Though my family moved a lot while I was growing up, the one constant in my life was art. I still carry that with me today, knowing that no matter what happens in life - that there is always art.

Many of my pieces focus on my interests ( which can vary wildly). I have always been fascinated and drawn to nature and animals and most of my work features animals, real and imaginary. I have interest in fantasy, science fiction, animation, movies, books, you name it! All of these interests play a big part in my art, and help to shape the stories that I tell.

As an artist, I have often been interested in many different mediums and subjects. I enjoy exploring new creative outlets and learning new ways to express my ideas through art! Currently, my pieces are mainly created through digital painting, acrylic painting, and watercolor. However, I also love sculpting using Cosclay and on the computer using programs like Blender3d.
