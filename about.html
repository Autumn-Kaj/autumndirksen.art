About Me

Hi! Thanks for visiting my art portfolio! I am an artist from the Pacific Northwest currently based in Seattle, WA. I specialize in digital illustration, character art, and concept work. I also dabble in traditional media when the mood suites me, and have become proficient in water-color and acrylic painting. I love animals and have a cat and a dog that I love dearly, and whom inspire me every day!
